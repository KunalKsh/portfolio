* **UNCERTAINITY** - PROBABILITY THEORY PROVIDES THE FRAMEWORK TO QUANTIFY UNCERTAINITY \& MODELING RANDOM PHENOMENA.



* **RANDOM VARIABLES** - IT IS A VARIABLE WHOSE VALUE IS DETERMINED BY THE OUTCOME OF A RANDOM EXPERIMENT OR PROCESS.

1. **DISCRETE RANDOM VARIABLE** - IT CAN TAKE A COUNTABLE NUMBER OF DISTINCT VALUES (NO. OF HEADS IN A SERIES OF COIN TOSSES)
2. **CONTINUOUS RANDOM VARIABLE** - IT CAN TAKE ANY VALUE WITHHIN A SPECIFIED RANGE OR INTERVAL IMPLYING AN INFINITE OF POSSIBLE VALUES (AVERAGE HEIGHT)

"PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTION FOR MODELING A BUSINESS PHENOMENA DEPENDS ENTIRELY ON IDENTIFYING WHETHER THE RANDOM VARIABLE IS DISCRETE OR CONTINUOUS"



PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTION -



1. **NORMAL DISTRIBUTION** (GAUSSIAN DISTRIBUTION) - BELL-SHAPED CURVE, THAT IS SYMMETRICAL AROUND IT'S MEAN.

      - 68.3% OF DATA FALLS WITHIN (+-1) STANDARD DEVIATION

      - 95.4% OF DATA FALLS WITHIN (+-2) STANDARD DEVIATION

      - 99.7% OF DATA FALSS WITHIN (+-3) STANDARD DEVIATION

EXAMPLE - FACTORY PRODUCING HELMETS OF DIFFERENT SIZE, FINANCIAL MARKET FOR IDENTIFYING UPTRENDS OR DOWNTRENDS, MODELING THE DISTRIBUTION OF PRODUCT DIMENSIONS



2\. **BINOMIAL DISTRIBUTION** - IT DESCRIBES THE SUCCESSES IN A FIXED NUMBER OF INDEPENDENT TRIALS, WHERE EACH TRIAL HAS ONLY TWO POSSIBLE OUTCOMES. DEPICTED USING BAR CHART

      - DISTRIBUTION IS SYMMETRIC WHEN P = 0.5

      - SKEWED WHEN P DEVIATES FROM 0.5

      - INCREASE IN NO. OF TRIAL INCREASES, THE BAR PLOT RESEMBLES BELL-SHAPED CURVE

EXAMPLE - CALCULATING THE PROBABILITY OF ABSENTEEISM AT WORKPLACE, PREDICTING THE CAMPAIGN SUCCESS, PROBABILITY OF FINDING SPECIFIC NO. OF DEFECTIVE ITEMS IN A BATCH
