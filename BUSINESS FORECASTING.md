**ARIMA STRUCTURES-**

CORRELATION BETWEEN VALUES OF A TIME SERIES IN NEIGHBOURING PERIODS IS CALLED AUTOCORRELATION AS IT DESCRIBES THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE SERIES \& ITSELF.

AUTOCORRELATION - CORRELATION BETWEEM THE SERIES \& A LAGGED VERSION OF THE SERIES.

A LAGGED SERIES IS A COPY OF THE ORIGINAL SERIES, WHICH IS MOVED FORWARD ONE OR MORE TIME PERIODS.

A LAGGED SERIES WITH LAG-1 IS THE ORIGINAL SERIES MOVED FORWARFD ONE TIME-PERIOD; A LAGGED SERIES WITH LAG-2 IS SERIES MOVED FORWARD TWO TIME PERIODS. Acf()



* STRONG AUTOCORRELATION(+ OR -) AT MULTIPLES OF A LAG LARGER THAN 1 IT REPRESENTS A SEASONAL PATTERN.
* POSITIVE LAG-1 AUTOCORRELATION(STICKNESS) - SERIES WHERE CONSECUTIVE VALUES MOVE GENERALLY IN THE SAME DIRECTION. STRONG LINEAR TREND - STRONG + LAG-1 AUTOCORRELATION
* NEGATIVE LAG-1 AUTOCORRELATION - REFLECTS SWINGS IN THE SERIES, WHERE HIGH VALUES ARE IMMEDIDATELY FOLLOWED BY LOW VALUES \& VICE VERSA.

EXAMINING THE AUTOCORRELATION OF A SERIES MAY HELP TO DETECT SEASONALITY PATTERNS.



**STATIONARITY-** TIME SERIES IS ONE WHOSE STATISTICAL PROPERTIES(EXPECTATION, VARIANCE, ACF) DO NOT DEPEND ON THE TIME AT WHICH THE SERIES IS OBSERVED.

WHITE NOISE SERIES IS STATIONARY. RANDOM WALK IS A SERIES WHOSE SUCCESSIVE DIFFERENCES ARE WHITE NOISE: THE SERIES IS NON-STATIONARY, BUT THE DIFFERENCES ARE STATIONARY.

TIME SERIES WITH TREND, OR WITH SEASONALITY, ARE NOT STATIONARY. TIME SERIES WITH CYCLIC BEHAVIOUR WITH NO TREND \& SEASONALITY IS TREATED AS STATIONARY FOR MODELING.

FOR STATIONARY TIME SERIES, THE ACF WILL DROP TO ZERO RELATIVELY QUICKLY, WHILE THE ACF OF NON-STATIONARY DATA DECREASES SLOWLY. VALUE OF r(1) IS OFTEN LARGE \& POSITIVE.
